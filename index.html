Limitations of BMI

Although BMI is often a practical indicator of helthy weight, it is not suited of every person. Specific groups should carefully consider their BMI outcomes, and in cartein cases, the measurement may not be beneficial to use.

Gender

The development and body fat composition of girls and boys vary with age. Consecuantly, a child's age and gender are considered ehen evaluating their BMI.

Age

In aging individuals, increased body fat and muscle loss may cause BMI to understimate body fat content.

Muscle

BMI may misclasify muscular individuals as overweight or obese,as it doesn't differentiate muscle from fat.

Pregnancy

Expectant mothers experience weight gain due to their growing baby. Maintaining a healthy pre-pregnancy BMI is advisable to minimise health risk for both mother and child.

Race

Certain health concern may affect individuals of some Black and Asian origins at lower BMIs than others. To learn more, it is advised to disscuss this with your GP or practicle nurse.
